What we do

Services, end to end.

Six core capabilities that cover the entire lifecycle of a mobile product — from the first sketch to the last dashboard chart after launch.

Service 01

Casual Game Development

End-to-end development of hyper-casual, puzzle, runner, arcade and lifestyle games. We build, port, soft-launch, A/B test and scale. Native iOS, native Android, and Unity — picked per project. We handle game design, art direction, sound design, gameplay engineering, and live-ops event tooling.

  • Concept-to-launch pipeline in 8–12 weeks
  • Soft-launch, A/B testing & analytics
  • Live-ops events, daily missions, retention loops
  • Multi-language localization (40+ locales)
  • Engines: Unity, Cocos, Native iOS/Android
Puzzle Runner Arcade Hyper-casual Mid-core
Service 02

Daily Utility Apps

Battery-friendly, fast-launching utility apps that people open every day. Flashlights, scanners, weather, calculators, file converters, photo tools, productivity aids. Privacy-first by default — no data harvested, no creepy tracking, no dark patterns.

  • On-device intelligence (CoreML, ML Kit)
  • Widget, Live Activity, Dynamic Island support
  • Subscription, IAP, and ad-supported models
  • App-shortcuts, Siri/Actions intents
  • Accessibility built in from day one (WCAG 2.2 AA)
Productivity Tools Photo Lifestyle
Service 03

Custom Mobile Management Applications

Bespoke mobile management applications for distributed teams. Field service, fleet, inventory, CRM, internal dashboards — secure, offline-capable, role-based. We deliver native iOS & Android apps that integrate with your existing back-office, ERP, or SaaS.

  • Native iOS & Android, plus tablet layouts
  • SSO via OIDC, SAML, Magic Link, OAuth 2.0
  • Granular role-based access (RBAC) & device management
  • Offline-first sync with conflict resolution
  • Encrypted local storage, secure enclave / keystore
  • Audit trails, GDPR & SOC 2-ready logging
  • Push, in-app, SMS, email notification routing
Field Service Fleet Inventory CRM Internal Tools
Service 04

App Store Publishing & ASO

We've shipped dozens of titles through both Google Play and the Apple App Store, including appeals, expedited reviews, and the new privacy / age-rating regimes. We handle the entire submission cycle and keep your listings optimized.

  • Google Play Console & App Store Connect management
  • Privacy manifests (Apple), Data Safety form (Google)
  • Age ratings: IARC, ESRB, PEGI, USK, GSRR, CLASSIND
  • Store listing localization & creative testing
  • Search & conversion optimization (ASO)
  • App Review & Play Policy appeals
Google Play App Store ASO Compliance
Service 05

Ad Monetization & Mediation

We orchestrate ad stacks across every major network. We set up and tune AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, InMobi, Vungle, Chartboost, Digital Turbine, Liftoff, Tapjoy, Smaato, Amazon Publisher Services and Start.io — and structure the waterfall / bidding for your specific audience and region mix.

Ad formats we ship

  • Banner ads (standard, adaptive, collapsible)
  • Interstitial ads (static, video, playable)
  • Rewarded video ads (opt-in, server-side callback)
  • App-open / splash ads (with frequency capping)
  • Native ads (custom layouts, in-feed)
  • Playable & interactive end-cards

Compliance-first

  • GDPR consent management (TCF v2.2, IAB Europe)
  • CCPA / CPRA opt-out signals (USP API)
  • COPPA & UK Age-Appropriate Design Code flows
  • app-ads.txt & seller.json on every property
AdMob AppLovin Unity FAN Pangle + 10 more
Service 06

Live-Ops, Analytics & Growth

Launch is just the start. We run weekly content drops, seasonal events, A/B experiments, and a continuous ASO loop. Our analysts live in your retention dashboard — DAU, D1/D7/D30, ARPDAU, LTV, ROAS — and we move numbers, not slides.

  • Live-ops event design & implementation
  • A/B testing (Firebase Remote Config, internal)
  • Analytics & BI (Amplitude, Mixpanel, GA4, BigQuery)
  • Crash & performance (Crashlytics, Sentry, Datadog)
  • Store review & ratings management
  • Customer support tooling & macros
Live-Ops Analytics A/B Retention
