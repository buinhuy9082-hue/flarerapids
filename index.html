FAQ

Frequently asked.

Where are your apps published?

All our consumer apps are published on Google Play and the Apple App Store, in line with each platform's latest review guidelines (Google Play Policy 2024 and Apple App Review Guidelines 6.x). Enterprise mobile management applications are delivered through your organization's MDM channel — Apple Business Manager and Android Enterprise — and never appear on the public stores.

Which ad networks do you integrate?

We work with the major ad monetization platforms: Google AdMob, Google Ad Manager, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin (MAX), Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), Unity Ads, Pangle, Mintegral, InMobi, Vungle, Chartboost, Digital Turbine, Liftoff, Tapjoy, Smaato, Amazon Publisher Services and Start.io. Ad formats we commonly ship: banner, interstitial, rewarded video, native, app-open (splash) and playable. All ad SDKs are configured for COPPA, GDPR and CCPA consent before release.

How do you handle age ratings and child-safety policies?

Every title goes through an age-rating self-certification for IARC / ESRB / PEGI / USK / GSRR / CLASSIND and a target-audience declaration. We honor the Google Play Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category requirements where applicable, including the use of neutral age gates and the prohibition of personalized advertising to known minors. Read the full breakdown in our Privacy Policy.

Do you sign NDAs and work with enterprise procurement?

Yes — we sign mutual NDAs before any confidential exchange, accept standard enterprise purchase orders, and can invoice in USD, EUR or GBP. We support SOC 2-style vendor questionnaires, DPAs for GDPR Article 28, and Standard Contractual Clauses for cross-border transfers.

What's the typical engagement model?

Three models: (1) Fixed-scope for a clearly defined product, (2) Time & materials retainer for ongoing product teams, (3) Revenue-share for our own IP. All models include a one-week paid pilot so you can de-risk before committing.

Where is FlareRapids based, and do you serve the EU / UK?

FlareRapids is a remote-first global studio, with primary availability across US, EU and UK timezones. We contract under US law by default and can structure EU/UK entities for projects that require it.
